# Problems

The problems you choose to spend your career and life on, framed per [[80 - Telos]] and Miessler's [Plan Your Career Around Problems](https://danielmiessler.com/blog/plan-career-around-problems). Problems are the keystone of the framework: [[06 - Strategies|strategies]], [[02 - Mission|mission]], [[04 - Goals|goals]], and [[07 - Projects|projects]] all derive from them. They are the unit that survives industry shifts and AI-era job-title compression.

Three tiers: **world-scale** (civilizational stakes), **domain-scale** (bounded inside your specialty, where you can plausibly make dents), **personal-scale** (specific to you, your family, your craft).

## Personal-scale

### P4: concretizing expertise before it stales ^P4

**Statement.** Expertise accumulated over years has a half-life. If you don't crystallize it (writing, methodology, public artifacts), it expires with the role that produced it.

**Stakes.** Loss of leverage in the legacy axis; a body of work that never outlives any single job.

**What progress looks like.** Recurring published artifacts; methodology adopted beyond you; one or more "snapshot" works (a book, a public spec, a curated collection).

**Linked.** [[06 - Strategies#^S4|S4]].
