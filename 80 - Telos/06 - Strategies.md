# Strategies

The approaches you take to make progress on each [[01 - Problems|problem]]. Per [[80 - Telos]] and Miessler's framework, strategies should be **load-bearing** (decisions you can act on this week) and **defensible** (you can say why this approach over alternatives). They derive from [[01 - Problems]] and constrain [[04 - Goals]] and [[07 - Projects]].
