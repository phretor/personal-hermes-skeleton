# Ideas

Ideas that aren't yet projects live in **[[40 - Concepts]]**. Some embryonic ideas also live buried in `10 - Projects/<project>/notes/` if they belong to an existing thread.

## What counts as an idea worth its own page

An idea earns its own page in `40 - Concepts/` when:

1. It doesn't fit cleanly into an existing project's notes.
2. It might be revisited (the test: would the future-me likely want to find it again?).
3. It's worth more than a one-liner: has at least a framing paragraph or open questions worth recording.

Smaller fragments stay in the [[30 - Daily|daily notes]]. The weekly review surfaces the ones that recur, and those graduate to `40 - Concepts/`.

## Flow

1. **Capture**: in the daily note, in the relevant project's notes, or directly as a new file in `40 - Concepts/`.
2. **Promote**: when an idea has been written about more than once and starts to look like work, create a project directory in `10 - Projects/<name>/` and migrate.
3. **Retire**: ideas that haven't been touched in a year (and don't survive the year-end Telos review) move to `ZZ - Archive/` within `40 - Concepts/`.

## How this file is used

This Telos slot is navigation. The idea inventory lives in [[40 - Concepts]].

- **Quarterly review**: sweep `40 - Concepts/` for ideas that have aged into either a project or retirement.
- **Yearly review**: full audit. Keep the active surface small. An overflowing concept folder is a sign of capture without curation.
