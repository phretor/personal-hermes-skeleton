# Narratives

The internal stories I tell myself about who I am and what I can / can't do. They sit between [[02 - Mission|Mission]] (who I want to become) and `[[14 - Habits]]` (what I do). The **reinforcing** ones I *speak*; they harden the architecture. The **sabotaging** ones I *name*; you can't contest a story you haven't seen.

Both belong in this file. So do the **counter-lines**: the specific words I say back to a sabotaging story when I catch it running, anchored to a reinforcer that earns the rebuttal.
